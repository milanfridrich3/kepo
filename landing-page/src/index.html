You own more than you can keep in your head

Most of what we own sits in drawers, closets and other people’s homes. Memory is a poor place to keep it.

  • You lent a tool to a friend and can’t remember who has it.
  • The warranty ran out the month before the laptop broke.
  • You know you own it. You just don’t know which box, shelf or room it’s in.

Everything about your things, in one place

Kepo keeps it simple: your items, where they are, and who has them.

  • Items

    Add what you own with a photo, category, purchase date, value, serial number and notes.

  • Locations

    Organise places inside places — Home, Bedroom, Desk — and always see where an item lives.

  • Lending

    Record who has something, since when and when it’s due back. Overdue loans are flagged.

  • Warranties

    See which warranties are active, ending within 30 days, or already expired.

  • Search

    Find anything by name, category, location, borrower, notes or serial number.

  • Dashboard

    One overview of your items, what’s lent out, what needs attention and recent activity.
