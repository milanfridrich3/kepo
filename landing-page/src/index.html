Why Kepo

You own more than you can keep in your head.

Most of what we own sits in drawers, closets and other people’s homes. Memory is a poor place to keep it.

  • Lent out

    You lent a tool to a friend and can’t remember who has it.

    Record who has it, since when and when it’s due back.

  • Warranty

    The warranty ran out the month before the laptop broke.

    See which warranties are active, ending soon or expired.

  • Storage

    You know you own it. You just don’t know which box, shelf or room it’s in.

    Give every item a place and find it by searching.
